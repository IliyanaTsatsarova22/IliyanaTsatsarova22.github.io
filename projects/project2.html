School project 2 - Cooking game

An interactive cooking simulation game where players must complete various dishes by following the correct recipe steps under pressure.

  • Steps & Order: Complete each dish by tapping steps in the correct order.
  • Lives System: Start with 3 lives. A wrong step costs 1 life and skips to the next dish!
  • Interactive Buttons: Buttons get disabled and change color upon clicking.
  • Progression: Advance levels by finishing all dishes with at least 1 life remaining.
