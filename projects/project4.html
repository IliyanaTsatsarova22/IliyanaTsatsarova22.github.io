School project 4 - collectables

An interactive web application where users can manage their own digital collections and items, and explore what other users are collecting.

  • Authentication: Secure login and registration system.
  • Collection Management: Create, edit, and organize your own collections.
  • Item Management: Add and manage specific items within your collections.
  • Explore & Search: Browse other users existing collections and search through items.
